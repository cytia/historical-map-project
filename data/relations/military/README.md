# Military relations

`command-structure.json` stores military subordination, military affiliation, and Five Army affiliation.

`administrative-context.json` stores the administrative unit or region associated with a military unit's seat or documented context. It does not mean that the military unit is subordinate to that administrative unit.

`co-location.json` stores separately evidenced cases where a military and administrative institution share a physical place. A shared name or nearby coordinate is not sufficient evidence for this relation.
